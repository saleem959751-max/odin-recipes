Description

This homemade pizza is simple, cheesy, and perfect for a quick dinner. The crispy crust and gooey toppings make it a family favorite.

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza base
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup sliced bell peppers
  • 1/4 cup sliced olives
