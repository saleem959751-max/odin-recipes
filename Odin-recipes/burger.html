burger


Description

This homemade burger is quick, tasty, and perfect for lunch or dinner. With a juicy patty and fresh toppings, it’s a classic comfort food.


Ingredients

  • 2 burger buns
  • 200g ground beef patty
  • 2 slices of cheese
  • 4 slices of tomato
  • Lettuce leaves
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise or ketchup

Steps

  1. Toast the burger buns lightly.
  2. Cook the beef patty on a skillet until done.
  3. Place cheese on the patty and let it melt.
  4. Spread mayonnaise or ketchup on the buns.
  5. Layer lettuce and tomato on the bottom bun.
  6. Add the patty with cheese, cover with the top bun, and serve.
